Recipe: Pizza Dip

Delicious pull-apart garlic bread with warm cheesy dip

Ingredients: Pull-Apart Bread

  • 16.3 oz can Grands biscuit dough (8 count)
  • ¼ cup melted butter
  • 2 tsp parmesan cheese, grated
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning

Step-by-Step Instructions

Step 1-3: Preheat oven to 350°F. Cut biscuits in half, roll into balls, and mix melted butter, parmesan, and 1 tsp Italian seasoning.

Step 4: Roll dough balls in seasoned butter and arrange around a large cast iron skillet.

Step 5: Beat cream cheese, ricotta, and 2 tsp Italian seasoning together; spread in center of skillet.

Step 6: Top with marinara sauce evenly over the cheese mixture.

Step 7: Sprinkle shredded mozzarella cheese and remaining seasoning over sauce.

Step 8: Top with quartered pepperoni slices.

Step 9: Bake in oven for 25-30 minutes until golden and bubbling.

Ingredients: Pizza Dip

  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 3 tsp Italian seasoning, divided
  • 1 cup marinara or pizza sauce
  • 1 ½ cups mozzarella cheese, grated
  • 8 slices pepperoni, quartered
